Programs That Move You Forward

Start with an industry-aligned curriculum and finish with hands-on experience.

BTech

Bachelor of Technology

Build future-proof engineering skills with a curriculum that blends rigorous academics, hands-on projects, and a compulsory Industry Practice in the final year—so you graduate work-ready and research-aware. 

Duration: 4 Years 

  • 6-month Industry Practice (IP) in the final semester with real projects and joint industry–faculty mentoring.
  • Project-based, industry-linked learning across all eight semesters.
  • Options to deepen via research practice/extended IP tracks.

Available courses 

  • Btech with Streams : Computer Science & Engineering (CSE), Artificial Intelligence & Data Science , Cyber Security ,Electronics & Communication Engineering (ECE) ,Robotics & Automation ,Biotechnology
BBA

Bachelor of Business Administration

A contemporary business degree designed around entrepreneurship, analytics, and global business practice—with the flexibility to specialise and the option of international/industry immersion.

Duration: 3 Years 

  • Specialisations in FinTech, Digital Marketing, Family-Owned Business. 
  • Case-based, applied coursework with leadership training. 
  • Optional international/industry immersion to strengthen employability. 

Available courses 

  • Other BBA Programmes : BBA in AI, BBA in Business Analytics
iMBA

Integrated MBA (Dual-degree, BBA+MBA)

A single, 5-year pathway that starts you early on management fundamentals and culminates in an MBA with focused specialisation—backed by capstones and an Industry Practice semester.

Duration: 5 Years

  • Early foundation in business + advanced MBA coursework. 
  • Choose MBA specialisations in Digital Marketing, Business Analytics, or FinTech. 
  • Structured for experiential learning and outcome-driven projects (including Industry Practice).

Available courses 

  • iMBA with specialisations in: Digital Marketing, Business Analytics, FinTech.
